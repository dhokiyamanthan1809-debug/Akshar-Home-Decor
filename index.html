About the Showroom

One showroom, every finishing touch your home needs.

Akshar Home Decor brings doors, frames, hardware, plywood and modular interiors together in a single showroom in Jamnagar, so you're not running between five different shops to finish one room.

Whether you're building a new home or replacing a single door handle, our team helps you pick the right material, the right size, and a price that's clear from the first visit.
